# VeriWorkly Portfolio — Hosting & Pricing Tiers

## Free Tier

- **Price**: $0
- **Includes**:
  - Full Portfolio Editor (3-pane workspace)
  - 2 Free Templates: Signal (Engineering Bento) & Atelier (Design Editorial)
  - Shared Path Hosting: `portfolio.veriworkly.com/portfolio/your-slug`
  - Floating "Built with VeriWorkly" Watermark

## Creator Pro Subdomain Plan

- **Price**: $9.99 / month or $7.99 / month ($95.88 billed annually) (or included in Sprint Pass $2.99, Hunt Pass $5.99, or Monthly Pro $14.99)
- **Includes**:
  - Custom Subdomain Hosting (`your-slug.veriworkly.com`)
  - All 4 Templates (Signal, Atelier, Nimbus, Cipher)
  - Multi-Page Portfolios (Work, Writing, About, Contact, Project Detail pages)
  - Full View Analytics & Referrer Source Breakdown
  - Watermark Removal Toggle
  - Custom SEO Title, Description, and Social Share Image Uploads

# VeriWorkly Portfolio — Pricing

> Structured pricing reference for human visitors and AI agents. All prices in USD. The same
> plans and prices as https://veriworkly.com/pricing.md.

> **Status**: payments are currently closed, so none of the paid plans below can be bought
> today; the /pricing page shows the same notice. Portfolio publishing is not yet enabled in
> production. The tiers below describe the model at launch.

Every portfolio, free or paid, is published on its own `your-slug.veriworkly.com` subdomain.
The plan decides the templates, the badge, analytics, SEO controls and extra pages, never the
address.

## Plans

### 1. Free Tier

- **Price**: $0
- **Includes**:
  - The full portfolio editor (3-pane workspace with live preview)
  - The free templates: Signal and Atelier
  - Publishing on your own `your-slug.veriworkly.com` subdomain
  - A small "Built with VeriWorkly" badge on the published site

### 2. 3-Day Sprint Pass

- **Price**: $2.99 (one time, does not renew)
- **Includes**: 3 days of Creator Pro portfolio features and 150 AI credits (valid during the pass)

### 3. 7-Day Hunt Pass

- **Price**: $5.99 (one time, does not renew)
- **Includes**: 7 days of Creator Pro portfolio features and 400 AI credits (valid during the pass)

### 4. Creator Pro

- **Price**: $9.99 / month, or $95.88 / year, which is $7.99 / month (save 20%)
- **Includes**:
  - All four templates: Signal, Atelier, Nimbus and Cipher
  - Multi-page portfolios (Work, Writing, About, Contact and project detail pages)
  - Visitor analytics: views, trend and referring sites
  - Badge removal
  - SEO title, description and social share image
  - No AI credits

### 5. AI Credits

- **Price**: $5.99 / month, or $59.88 / year, which is $4.99 / month (save 17%)
- **Includes**: 1,000 AI credits a month. AI credits only: your portfolio stays on the free tier.

### 6. Job Hunter Bundle

- **Price**: $14.99 / month, or $143.88 / year, which is $11.99 / month (save 20%)
- **Includes**: everything in Creator Pro, plus 1,000 AI credits a month.

## Credits

- Subscription credits are granted 1,000 a month on monthly and annual plans alike, reset each
  month and do not roll over.
- One credit balance is shared across every VeriWorkly app.

### Credit packs (one time)

- **250 extra credits**: $2.99, valid for 90 days
- **500 extra credits**: $4.99, valid for 90 days
- **1,200 extra credits**: $9.99, valid for 90 days

Packs buy AI credits only; they never unlock portfolio features.

## Changing or cancelling

- Subscriptions renew until you cancel them in the billing portal. You keep paid features until
  the end of the period you paid for.
- A Creator Pro or AI Credits subscriber can upgrade to the Job Hunter Bundle at any time from
  the billing page. The billing period stays the same (monthly stays monthly, annual stays
  annual) and you pay only the difference for the rest of that period.
- Any other change (a smaller plan, or monthly to annual and back) means cancelling in Manage
  billing and subscribing again when the current period ends.
- Passes and credit packs are one-time purchases.
- When paid access ends, the portfolio gets 7 days of grace, then moves to the free tier
  (Nimbus becomes Atelier, Cipher becomes Signal, the badge returns, paid SEO settings are
  dropped). It stays online on its subdomain.

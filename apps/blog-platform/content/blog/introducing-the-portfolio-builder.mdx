A resume gets you past a recruiter's first pass. A portfolio is what convinces them to reach out. This post introduces VeriWorkly's Portfolio builder and says plainly what you can do with it today.

## What it is

Portfolio is a public website builder that lives alongside the resume and cover letter editor: a template gallery, a three-pane editor (structure, content and a live preview), a dashboard, and the published sites themselves. Once publishing opens at launch, every portfolio, free or paid, is published on its own subdomain, `your-name.veriworkly.com`.

## Four templates: two free, two paid

- **Signal** (free)
- **Atelier** (free)
- **Nimbus** (paid, at launch)
- **Cipher** (paid, at launch)

## The editor

A three-pane workspace (section list, content, live preview) that autosaves every 12 seconds. It supports 18 section types: projects, experience, education, services, skills, writing, testimonials, awards, certifications, languages, interests, publications, patents, test scores, achievements, volunteer, custom and contact.

## Free and paid

- **Free:** the Signal and Atelier templates, published on your own `your-name.veriworkly.com` subdomain, with a "Built with VeriWorkly" badge on the page.
- **Paid, at launch:** the Nimbus and Cipher templates, the option to remove the badge, visitor analytics and SEO controls. The plan only changes these; every portfolio gets the same kind of `your-name.veriworkly.com` subdomain. If a paid plan lapses, the portfolio switches to a free template with the badge and stays online on its subdomain. There are no custom domains.

Plans and prices are on [veriworkly.com/pricing](https://veriworkly.com/pricing).

## Where things stand today

You can sign in, open the editor and build and preview a complete portfolio today. Publishing is admin-only in production for now, so no one else can put a portfolio live yet, on any plan. Payments are in the same state. Both open at launch.

The portfolio template gallery is public at [portfolio.veriworkly.com/templates](https://portfolio.veriworkly.com/templates), no sign-in needed.

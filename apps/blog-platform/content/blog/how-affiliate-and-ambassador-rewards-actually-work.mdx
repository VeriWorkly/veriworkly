VeriWorkly has two growth programs, Affiliate and Ambassador, and they are at different stages. Here is where each one stands today.

One fact covers both: commissions are earned only on purchases, and purchases are admin-only in production today. Until payments open at launch, nobody earns a commission. Both programs are also off by default in production and are switched on when we launch them.

## Affiliate: built, waiting on payments

The Affiliate program has a dashboard in Studio covering tiers, referrals, commissions, payouts and a public leaderboard.

**Commission structure** is a 3-tier rate paid on every payment a referred customer makes, renewals included: Starter at 2% (0+ conversions), Growth at 3% (10+ conversions), Partner at 5% (50+ conversions). Promotion is automatic: a conversion counts towards your tier once its commission has been released, and a tier is never lowered automatically. Commissions start as pending, because a purchase can still be refunded, and a person on our team releases them.

**Payouts** work through Dodo Payments, our billing provider, which does not offer an automated payout API. Payouts are sent out-of-band (bank transfer, for example) and recorded by hand once sent. You need at least $25 available before you can request a withdrawal.

A few things worth knowing if you are considering joining: signing someone up earns nothing on its own, only their payments do. Self-referral is blocked, and each account can have one referral for life. A referral code has to be applied within 30 days of the referred person signing up. Once applied, the referral does not expire.

## Ambassador: application intake only

The Ambassador program is newer, and it launches the same way Affiliate did: application intake first, rewards later.

**What exists today:** you can submit an application with your college name and graduation year, and check its status. A person on our team reads each application and approves it or turns it down. There is no automated `.edu` check. The Studio page shows four states: not yet applied, pending review, accepted, and not accepted this round.

**What does not exist yet:** a points system, vouchers, or any other ambassador reward. Nothing is earned for invites or content today.

If you apply now, you are joining a program at its intake stage, not one with a settled rewards table.

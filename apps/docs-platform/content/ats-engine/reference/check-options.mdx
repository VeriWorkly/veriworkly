`check(resume, policy = DEFAULT_POLICY, options)` returns an [`AtsReport`](/ats-engine/reference/report).
The tables are read from the engine's own type declarations (engine 0.22.0). Every option is
optional, and each accepts `undefined` as absent. `AtsFileInfo` and `AtsLayoutSignals` are the
shapes of the `file` and `layout` options; `extractResume` from `@veriworkly/ats-engine/node`
returns the layout signals of an uploaded file.

## `AtsLayoutSignals`

Geometry recovered while parsing an uploaded document, for checks that text cannot answer. `columnRatio` is the share of content lines split by a gutter-wide gap (a two-column layout, sidebar or text box); `tableCount` the ruled table grids traced from the page's drawing operators. Absent for pasted text and structured documents; the rules that depend on it are then omitted rather than assumed to pass.

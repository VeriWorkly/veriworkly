  Returns the caller's plan, subscription status, entitlements, AI credit
  wallet, and public

  catalog in one call. Entitlements (e.g. portfolio publishing) and the AI
  credit wallet are

  two independent systems — a plan can grant one, both, or neither depending on
  `productKey`.

  them month by

  month, not all at once. Subscription credits expire at the end of their month
  and never roll

  over; credit packs last 90 days. `graceEndsAt` and `publicationStatus`
  describe the grace

  period after a failed renewal, and `access` tells a paying customer from a
  free one.

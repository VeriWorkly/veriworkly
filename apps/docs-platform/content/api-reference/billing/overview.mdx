## Description

Billing is split across two independent systems that are easy to conflate:

- **Entitlements** (`ai_credits`, `portfolio_publish`, `seo_controls`, `analytics`,
  `watermark_removal`) gate feature access. They come from a plan: `ai_credits` (AI Credits),
  `portfolio_pro` (Creator Pro), or `bundle` (Job Hunter Bundle, also sold as the 3-Day Sprint Pass
  and the 7-Day Hunt Pass).
- **The AI credit wallet** is a separate, spendable balance used by `/ai/generate`, the AI ATS
  endpoints, and the LinkedIn import. There is one pool, whatever the source.

How credits arrive:

- AI Credits and the Job Hunter Bundle grant 1,000 credits each month, on monthly and annual plans
  alike. An annual plan grants them month by month, not 12,000 up front. Unused subscription
  credits expire at the end of each month and do not roll over.
- The 3-Day Sprint Pass grants 150 credits and the 7-Day Hunt Pass 400.
- Credit packs (250, 500, or 1,200 credits) are one-time purchases. Their credits last 90 days and
  add AI credits only; a pack unlocks no other feature.
- Creator Pro grants no credits.

There are no free trials on any plan.

`GET /billing/me` returns entitlements and the wallet in one summary, along with the public product
catalog.

Checkout and portal sessions are created through **Dodo Payments**. `POST /billing/checkout`
validates `productKey` and `interval` against the product catalog before handing off to Dodo, and
rejects any other combination with `400`. A checkout that clashes with what the account already has
answers `409` with a `details.code`: `CHECKOUT_IN_PROGRESS` (another checkout is still open; cancel
it with `POST /billing/checkout/cancel`), `ALREADY_SUBSCRIBED`, `ALREADY_INCLUDED`,
`SUBSCRIPTION_OVERLAP`, `UPGRADE_AVAILABLE`, `SUBSCRIPTION_PAUSED`, or `PAYMENT_OUTSTANDING`. For
the codes about an existing subscription, `details` also carries `activeProductKey` and
`suggestedProductKey`; `CHECKOUT_IN_PROGRESS` carries only `code`.

The only plan change the API carries out is an upgrade of AI Credits or Creator Pro to the Job
Hunter Bundle at the same billing interval. Any other change answers `409`
(`PLAN_CHANGE_NOT_UPGRADE`, `PLAN_CHANGE_INTERVAL`, `ALREADY_SUBSCRIBED`, `SUBSCRIPTION_ENDING`,
and others): to move to a smaller plan, cancel from the billing portal and subscribe again when
the period ends. Preview the upgrade with `POST /billing/subscription/change-plan/preview`, then
call
`POST /billing/subscription/change-plan` with an `Idempotency-Key` header (or an `idempotencyKey` body field).

<Callout type="warn" title="Payments are admin-only in production for now">
  In production, subscription checkout, plan changes, and credit pack checkout answer `403` with
  `details.code: "PAYMENTS_DISABLED"` for every account except the configured admin. This does not
  apply in development.
</Callout>

## Authentication

All routes in this group require a session cookie — none accept an API key.

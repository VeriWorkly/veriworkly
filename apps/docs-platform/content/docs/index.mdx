**VeriWorkly is a privacy-first career workspace: a resume and cover letter builder, a portfolio
website builder, an ATS checker and an AI writing assistant, all fed by one Master Profile. You can
start without an account, no login required. Signing in adds cloud sync and share links.** Open it
at [app.veriworkly.com](https://app.veriworkly.com).

![VeriWorkly Banner](/og/landing-page-og.png)

## What is VeriWorkly?

VeriWorkly keeps your career facts in one place and turns them into every document and portfolio
you need. Documents are written to your browser's `localStorage` first, so nothing is lost offline
or before you sign in. With an account, cloud sync is on by default, and you can turn it off.

## Key Features

<Accordions>
  <Accordion id="free-open-source" title="Free & Open Source (MIT)">
    VeriWorkly is licensed under the MIT License. You can use it for free, modify it, and host your
    own instance. The codebase is on [GitHub](https://github.com/VeriWorkly/veriworkly). The four
    portfolio templates are the one exception: see [the private template
    submodule](/docs/architecture/monorepo#the-private-template-submodule).
  </Accordion>

<Accordion id="no-account" title="No Account Required to Start">
  Open Studio, choose **Continue as Guest** on the sign-in screen, and start writing. A 30-day guest
  cookie keeps you in guest mode, and your work is saved in your browser, so it survives a page
  reload without an account. Sign in for cloud sync and share links.
</Accordion>

<Accordion id="master-profile" title="One Master Profile, Many Documents">
  Every new resume or cover letter auto-seeds from your Master Profile, and the portfolio editor can
  import it. Editing a document never writes back to it, so you can tailor a resume to one job
  without changing your canonical data. See [Master Profile](/docs/product/master-profile).
</Accordion>

<Accordion id="rich-customization" title="Rich Customization">
  Adjust accent and text colours, fonts, section spacing, page padding, and line heights. Show,
  hide, and reorder sections per document.
</Accordion>

  <Accordion id="self-hostable" title="Self-Hosting Ready">
    Deploy the marketing site, API, and Redis with the provided Docker Compose configuration and an
    external PostgreSQL instance. See [Docker Deployment](/docs/getting-started/docker-deployment).
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>

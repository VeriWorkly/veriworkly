## Publishing

| Portfolio           | Published at                 |
| :------------------ | :--------------------------- |
| **Every portfolio** | `{your-slug}.veriworkly.com` |

A premium portfolio is a premium template (Nimbus or Cipher) published by an account with the
`portfolio_publish` entitlement (Creator Pro, the Job Hunter Bundle, or a pass). That same paid
access, not the template, decides the badge and extra pages.

The portfolio app's `proxy.ts` middleware rewrites the subdomain host onto an internal route; the
`/portfolio/{your-slug}` and `/user/{your-slug}` alias paths on `portfolio.veriworkly.com` also resolve
to it.

- **Multi-page access is enforced at the routing layer**, not just in the UI. Sub-page routes are
  served on any template while the owner has paid portfolio access (grace included), and return
  `404` otherwise.
- **Premium templates gate at publish time.** Publishing (or re-publishing after an edit) with
  Nimbus or Cipher is refused without the `portfolio_publish` entitlement.
- **Unpublishing** takes the site down while leaving the draft intact.

<Callout type="warn" title="Publishing opens at launch">
  Independent of plan, `PortfolioController.publish` rejects non-admin accounts with a `403` in
  production until launch. Dashboard access, draft editing, previews, and unpublishing are open to
  everyone. See [Portfolio: Overview](/docs/product/portfolio/overview).
</Callout>

### Publication lifecycle

A `PortfolioPublication` record holds one of three statuses:

| Status      | Meaning                                                                                                                         |
| :---------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `LIVE`      | Publicly served.                                                                                                                |
| `GRACE`     | A portfolio plan's renewal failed or was paused; the site stays up during the grace period (`PORTFOLIO_GRACE_DAYS`, default 7). |
| `SUSPENDED` | Not served: unpublished by the owner, taken down by a moderator, or suspended by billing.                                       |

When paid access ends for good (after the grace period, at the end of a cancelled plan's paid
period, or when a pass ends), the hourly portfolio access job moves a premium portfolio to the
closest free template (Nimbus to Atelier, Cipher to Signal). It stays `LIVE` with the badge.

Publishing stores a **content snapshot** and the published revision number, so continuing to edit the
draft does not change the live site until you publish again. Visitors get only the visible part of
that snapshot: hidden sections are left out, extra pages only while the owner has paid access, and
the email only when a visible Contact section shows it.

Published pages are cached for up to an hour. The API refreshes a site's page as soon as it is
published, unpublished, taken down, deleted, or changed by billing, through the portfolio app's
`/api/revalidate` hook (`PORTFOLIO_REVALIDATE_SECRET`). If the API cannot be reached, the last good
page keeps being served.

## Dashboard

Shows all-time views, visible section and project counts, and a **readiness percentage**.

A **status card** (also on Settings) says where the site stands: not published yet, live, in grace
until a date, offline because you unpublished it or after a billing change (with Republish), or
taken down by a moderator (with a support link and no Republish). It also shows the date a
cancelled plan ends. The reason comes from `offlineReason` on `GET /portfolios/me`. A live site can
be unpublished from the card, after a confirmation.

Publishing always applies the plan in effect at that moment. During grace that is the free plan, so
the editor asks first: publishing then brings the badge back and turns off Nimbus, Cipher, SEO
controls and extra pages. Renewing first keeps them.

Readiness is the share of a checklist that is complete:

1. Profile details: name, headline, bio and email all filled in.
2. Portfolio sections: at least two visible sections.
3. Search metadata: SEO title **and** description both set. Counted only with paid portfolio
   access, since free accounts cannot edit it.
4. Public publishing: the portfolio is publicly visible.

Each item is an equal share (25% each with paid access, a third each without). The score and the
checklist come from the same calculation (`apps/portfolio/lib/portfolio-status.ts`), so they always
agree.

## Analytics

Total views, a last-30-calendar-days trend, active days, and the top 10 referrers, aggregated daily
per publication and referrer host.

**Premium-gated** (the `analytics` entitlement), with two distinct locked states:

- A **logged-out** visitor sees a "Log In" prompt.
- A **logged-in free** user sees a "Visitor analytics is a premium feature" overlay with a link to
  the plans. The server sends no view data to an account without the entitlement.

If the analytics request fails for an account that has access, the page says analytics are
unavailable right now instead of showing the upgrade prompt.

<Callout type="info" title="View counting is deduplicated">
  Repeat views from the same IP within a 30-minute window are ignored before any counter increments,
  so refreshing your own portfolio does not inflate your numbers. Counts are buffered in Redis and
  flushed to PostgreSQL every 10 minutes, so very recent views can lag slightly.
</Callout>

## Settings

The subdomain/slug editor (with an inline format hint), SEO meta title and description, and social
share image upload. For a signed-in user the subdomain field checks availability when you leave it
(available, taken, or not allowed) and offers a suggestion when the name cannot be used.

The SEO fields and the image upload are **disabled for free users**, with a banner naming the
locked fields, and editable for accounts with the `portfolio_publish` entitlement. The subdomain
editor is open on every plan. The server re-checks at publish time: without
the `seo_controls` entitlement the published SEO block is replaced with defaults, whatever the draft
says.

**Save settings** saves only the draft; it never publishes. On a live site it then says "Saved.
Publish to update your live site." with a Publish action (left out while publishing is admin-only
in production). The header shows the same save state as the editor.

### Image uploads

Social share images and other portfolio assets upload through presigned Cloudflare R2 URLs valid for
10 minutes, followed by a verify step that `HEAD`-checks the uploaded object's size, content type,
and ETag against what was declared. Only JPG, PNG, and WebP are accepted, capped at 5 MB.

## Watermark

A "Built with VeriWorkly" badge appears on every free published portfolio. An account with paid
portfolio access (the `watermark_removal` entitlement) gets a per-portfolio toggle to remove it, on
any template, free or premium. Document exports (PDF, DOCX and the rest) never carry a watermark on any plan.

## Publishing

| Portfolio   | Published at                                                                                         |
| :---------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Free**    | A shared path: `portfolio.veriworkly.com/portfolio/{your-slug}` (`/user/{your-slug}` also resolves). |
| **Premium** | A subdomain: `{your-slug}.veriworkly.com`.                                                           |

A premium portfolio is a premium template (Nimbus or Cipher) published by an account with the
`portfolio_publish` entitlement (Creator Pro, the Job Hunter Bundle, or a pass).

Both forms are handled by the portfolio app's `proxy.ts` middleware, which rewrites the subdomain
host and the shared path onto the same internal route.

- **Subdomain access is enforced at the routing layer**, not just in the UI. A request to the
  subdomain of a free portfolio is server-redirected to the shared path, and multi-page slug routes
  return `404` on a free portfolio.
- **Premium templates gate at publish time.** Publishing (or re-publishing after an edit) with
  Nimbus or Cipher is refused without the `portfolio_publish` entitlement.
- **Unpublishing** takes the site down while leaving the draft intact.

<Callout type="warn" title="Publishing opens at launch">
  Independent of plan, `PortfolioController.publish` rejects non-admin accounts with a `403` in
  production until launch. Dashboard access, draft editing, previews, and unpublishing are open to
  everyone. See [Portfolio: Overview](/docs/product/portfolio/overview).
</Callout>

### Publication lifecycle

A `PortfolioPublication` record holds one of three statuses:

| Status      | Meaning                                                                                                                         |
| :---------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `LIVE`      | Publicly served.                                                                                                                |
| `GRACE`     | A portfolio plan's renewal failed or was paused; the site stays up during the grace period (`PORTFOLIO_GRACE_DAYS`, default 7). |
| `SUSPENDED` | Not served: unpublished by the owner, taken down by a moderator, or suspended by billing.                                       |

When paid access ends for good (after the grace period, at the end of a cancelled plan's paid
period, or when a pass ends), the hourly portfolio access job moves a premium portfolio to the
closest free template (Nimbus to Atelier, Cipher to Signal). It stays `LIVE` with the badge.

Publishing stores a **content snapshot** and the published revision number, so continuing to edit the
draft does not change the live site until you publish again.

## Dashboard

Shows all-time views, published section and project counts, and a **readiness percentage**.

Readiness is the share of a four-item checklist that is complete:

1. Profile details: name, headline, bio and email all filled in.
2. Portfolio sections: at least two visible sections.
3. Search metadata: SEO title **and** description both set.
4. Public publishing: the portfolio is publicly visible.

Each item is worth 25%. The score and the checklist come from the same calculation
(`apps/portfolio/lib/portfolio-status.ts`), so they always agree.

## Analytics

Total views, a recent-window trend, active days, and a referrer breakdown, aggregated daily per
publication and referrer host.

**Premium-gated** (the `analytics` entitlement), with two distinct locked states:

- A **logged-out** visitor sees a "Log In" prompt.
- A **logged-in free** user sees an "Upgrade to Pro" overlay. The server sends no view data to an
  account without the entitlement.

<Callout type="info" title="View counting is deduplicated">
  Repeat views from the same IP within a 30-minute window are ignored before any counter increments,
  so refreshing your own portfolio does not inflate your numbers. Counts are buffered in Redis and
  flushed to PostgreSQL every 10 minutes, so very recent views can lag slightly.
</Callout>

## Settings

The subdomain/slug editor (with an inline format hint), SEO meta title and description, and social
share image upload.

All are **disabled for free users**, with a banner naming the locked fields, and editable for
accounts with the `portfolio_publish` entitlement. The server re-checks at publish time: without
the `seo_controls` entitlement the published SEO block is replaced with defaults, whatever the draft
says.

### Image uploads

Social share images and other portfolio assets upload through presigned Cloudflare R2 URLs valid for
10 minutes, followed by a verify step that `HEAD`-checks the uploaded object's size, content type,
and ETag against what was declared. Only JPG, PNG, and WebP are accepted, capped at 5 MB.

## Watermark

A floating "Built with VeriWorkly" badge appears on every free published portfolio. On a premium
portfolio, an account with the `watermark_removal` entitlement gets a per-portfolio toggle to remove
it. Document exports (PDF, DOCX and the rest) never carry a watermark on any plan.

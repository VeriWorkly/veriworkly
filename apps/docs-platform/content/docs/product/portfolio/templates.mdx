**VeriWorkly has four portfolio website templates. Signal and Atelier are free: anyone can publish
them, with a "Built with VeriWorkly" badge. Nimbus and Cipher are premium: anyone can select and
preview them, but publishing one needs a paid portfolio plan or a pass, available at launch.** Browse
them in the [template gallery](https://portfolio.veriworkly.com/templates).

| Template    | Access         |
| :---------- | :------------- |
| **Signal**  | Free (default) |
| **Atelier** | Free           |
| **Nimbus**  | **Premium**    |
| **Cipher**  | **Premium**    |

Every template is rendered on the server, so a published portfolio loads as a normal web page.

---

## Which portfolio templates are free?

Signal and Atelier. A portfolio on either one publishes on its own `<name>.veriworkly.com`
subdomain with the badge, and needs no paid plan once publishing opens at launch.

## What do the premium templates add?

A premium template published from a paid plan (Creator Pro, the Job Hunter Bundle, or a pass) gets:

- visitor analytics and SEO controls;
- a toggle to remove the "Built with VeriWorkly" badge;
- extra pages, up to 10, each with its own sections. See [Portfolio: Editor](/docs/product/portfolio/editor).

Premium gating is enforced when you publish, not just in the picker. Publishing, or re-publishing
after an edit, with Nimbus or Cipher is refused without the `portfolio_publish` entitlement. See
[Billing & Credits](/docs/product/billing-and-credits) for the plans.

<Callout type="warn" title="Publishing opens at launch">
  Publishing is admin-only in production today. Everyone can build and preview a portfolio on any
  template now. See [Portfolio: Publishing](/docs/product/portfolio/publishing).
</Callout>

## What happens to a premium portfolio when my plan ends?

It stays online. After any grace period it moves to the closest free template (Nimbus to Atelier,
Cipher to Signal), shows the badge, and stays on its `<name>.veriworkly.com` subdomain. Your draft is untouched.

## Can I use a custom domain?

No. Every published portfolio, free or paid, is served only on its own `<name>.veriworkly.com`
subdomain.

## Frequently asked questions

<Accordions>
  <Accordion id="try-premium" title="Can I try a premium portfolio template for free?">
    Yes. Any account, including guest mode, can select Nimbus or Cipher and preview a full portfolio
    on it. Only publishing a premium template needs a paid plan or a pass. You can switch to Signal
    or Atelier at any time and publish for free once publishing opens.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion id="switch-template" title="Do I lose content when I switch portfolio templates?">
    No. The template controls how the portfolio looks; your sections and items stay the same. All
    four templates read the same portfolio content, so you can switch and compare before you
    publish.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion id="need-portfolio" title="Do I need a portfolio website as well as a resume?">
    It depends on your field. Designers and developers often benefit, because a portfolio shows the
    work a resume can only describe. The trade-offs are covered in [Should you have a portfolio
    website?](https://blog.veriworkly.com/should-you-have-a-portfolio-website)
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>

A three-pane workspace: section structure, content, and a live preview rendered via a real `<iframe>` kept in sync over `postMessage`, with a mobile/tablet/desktop viewport-width toggle. Below the `lg` breakpoint the three panes become **Structure / Content / Preview** tabs (arrow keys move between them).

Autosave and the leave-page warning run for the whole workspace, so the dashboard and Settings are covered too: unsaved changes are saved every 12 seconds, leaving the editor saves what is pending at once, and closing the tab with unsaved changes asks first (a guest is never asked; the draft is kept in the browser). The save state is always visible: **Saved**, **Saving...**, **Unsaved changes**, **Not saved** (with the reason), or **Conflict** when another tab or device saved first. A conflict pauses autosave until you choose **Load cloud copy** or **Keep mine**. A refused save waits for your next edit instead of being resent.

The editor mirrors the server's limits, with a counter as a field nears its cap, checks the email when you leave the field, and stops the add buttons at 24 sections, 24 items and 10 pages with the reason shown. Deleting a section, item or page asks for confirmation. Before publishing, missing required details are listed with **Go there** links, and a server `400` with `details` is listed the same way.

## Section types

The editor supports **18 section types**, each reorderable, hideable, and independently editable:

```
projects, experience, education, services, skills, writing, testimonials,
awards, certifications, languages, interests, publications, patents,
testScores, achievements, volunteer, custom, contact
```

The canonical list is `portfolioSectionTypes` in `packages/profile-core`, re-exported by
`apps/portfolio/lib/portfolio.ts`. The server's save and publish validators read the same list, so
every type the editor offers can be saved and published. A portfolio holds at most 24 sections, each
with at most 24 items.

## Premium: multi-page portfolios

On a paid portfolio plan the editor's **Pages** panel adds extra pages (up to 10): predefined
pages such as Work, Writing, About and Contact, plus a detail page for any item in the Projects
section. Each page has its own independent section list. Sub-page URLs are served on any template
while the owner has paid portfolio access (grace included); otherwise they return `404`.

## The separate preview route

The editor's preview pane is a private route, `/preview/[documentId]` (`/preview/guest` before you
save), loaded in a frame. The editor sends your unsaved content to it as you type, and the browser
renders it with your chosen template. Preview pages are not indexed and are not the published site.
The **Preview** button, which opens the preview in a new tab, saves the draft first and says so if
that save failed.

Opening `/editor?template=<id>` (the gallery's "Use this template") applies a free template. A
premium template without paid access opens the template picker on it, showing what unlocks it. The
parameter is then removed from the address.

## Draft storage and merging

The editor keeps its own draft layer, independent of Studio's document sync engine. Only a guest
draft is kept in the browser; a signed-in draft lives in the account. On login you are asked
whether to bring a guest draft into your account (it replaces the draft there) or discard it, so
nothing moves into an account without the owner choosing it. If your session expires while you
edit, your unsaved changes are kept in the browser and offered back the same way after you log in.

If the API cannot be reached for a signed-in user, the workspace says it cannot reach your
workspace and offers Retry. It does not fall back to guest mode, and it does not save until your
draft has loaded.

Content is validated server-side on save against a Zod schema that also caps field lengths: for
example a 120-character name, a 240-character headline, a 1,600-character bio, at most 12 social
links, and at most 24 sections.

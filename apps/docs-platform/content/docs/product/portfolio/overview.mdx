**VeriWorkly's portfolio builder turns your career history into a personal portfolio website with
four templates and a three-pane editor, plus visitor analytics on a paid plan. Anyone can build and preview a portfolio
today, signed in or in guest mode. Publishing opens at launch: every portfolio, free or paid, publishes on its own
`<name>.veriworkly.com` subdomain.** Start at
[portfolio.veriworkly.com](https://portfolio.veriworkly.com).

## What is the portfolio builder?

It is a single Next.js application (`apps/portfolio`) serving three surfaces:

1. **Public marketing**: the landing page, template gallery, pricing, and FAQ.
2. **The workspace**: dashboard, editor, analytics, settings, and profile.
3. **The published portfolio sites themselves**: served on
   `{slug}.veriworkly.com` subdomains, one per published portfolio, free or paid.

A `proxy.ts` middleware routes all three: it gates workspace paths behind a session cookie or the
guest-mode cookie, and rewrites the subdomain host (and the `/portfolio/{slug}` and `/user/{slug}` alias paths, which
also resolve) onto the internal
`/portfolios/[username]/[[...slug]]` route.

## Can I publish my portfolio yet?

Not yet. The workspace is open today; publishing and payments open at launch.

- **Workspace** (dashboard, editor, analytics, settings, profile): open to every user in
  production, signed in or in guest mode. Anyone can build and preview a full portfolio today.
- **Publishing**: admin-only in production until launch. Draft editing, previews and unpublishing an
  already-live site are open to everyone.
- **Checkout/payments** on the Portfolio pricing page: admin-only in production until launch,
  opening alongside publishing.

Both use the same mechanism: a server-only `ADMIN_EMAIL` check (fail-closed: if `ADMIN_EMAIL` is
unset, nobody is treated as an admin).

## What is on the public portfolio site?

- A landing page and a full template gallery, with per-template long-form detail pages (design philosophy, style guide, FAQs, best-fit guidance) and a live read-only preview of each template with demo content.
- A pricing page, an FAQ page, per-template Open Graph image generation, and JSON-LD structured data.
- A sitemap that dynamically includes every live published portfolio.

Template preview pages behave like normal web pages: text selection, find-in-page and screen readers
all work.

## How does the portfolio builder relate to Studio?

Portfolio and Studio share the same account, session, and Master Profile. A login on either carries
to the other via a root-domain session cookie.

Portfolio's own `/profile` page is **read-only**: a summary of the identity fields already in the
portfolio draft. It does not own Master Profile management and links out to Studio for editing. See
[Master Profile](/docs/product/master-profile).

The editor can import your Master Profile into the portfolio draft, and portfolio copy generation
uses the same credit-metered AI assistant as Studio. See
[AI Writing Assistant](/docs/product/ai-writing-assistant).

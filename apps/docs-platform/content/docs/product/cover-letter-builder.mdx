**To write a cover letter in VeriWorkly, create a new cover letter in Studio, fill in the sender,
recipient and letter fields, pick one of five free templates, and export it as PDF, DOCX or four
other formats. A new letter fills your details from your Master Profile, and AI can draft it from a
pasted job post if you have credits.** Start one at [app.veriworkly.com](https://app.veriworkly.com).

## Which fields does a cover letter have?

The letter's shape is defined once, as `CoverLetterContent` in `packages/profile-core`, and shared
by Studio and the server:

| Group      | Fields                                                                                                    |
| :--------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Sender     | `senderName`, `senderTitle`, `senderEmail`, `senderPhone`, `senderWebsite`, `senderLocation`, `links`     |
| Recipient  | `date`, `recipientName`, `recipientTitle`, `companyName`, `companyLocation`                               |
| Letter     | `jobTitle`, `subject`, `greeting`, `opening`, `body`, `highlights`, `closing`, `signature`, `postscript`  |
| Appearance | Font, page margin, paragraph spacing, line height, accent, sidebar, page and text colors, hidden sections |

Studio adds three fields of its own: `jobPost` (the pasted job post, used as context for AI writing
and never printed), `language` (the exported HTML's language tag), and the page size.

## Which cover letter templates are there?

Five templates, all freely selectable with no plan-based lock:

| Template               | Id                   | Design                                                                                           |
| :--------------------- | :------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Professional**       | `professional`       | Formal letterhead, strong subject treatment, conservative recruiter-safe spacing.                |
| **VeriWorkly Special** | `veriworkly-special` | A branded two-column layout with an identity rail and numbered proof points.                     |
| **Minimalist**         | `minimalist`         | A full-width stacked masthead with no column split, whitespace, and small uppercase labels.      |
| **Executive**          | `executive`          | A centered double-rule masthead with a traditional dateline-over-address arrangement.            |
| **ATS Essential**      | `ats-essential`      | The classic left-aligned block letter: no column split anywhere, the most parser-safe structure. |

## How does the cover letter builder fit with the rest of VeriWorkly?

- **Seeding**: a new cover letter auto-seeds from your
  [Master Profile](/docs/product/master-profile) when created with no content.
- **Free-tier limit**: one active cover letter per account, independent of the one-resume limit.
  Both caps lift with either the `ai_credits` or `portfolio_publish` entitlement.
- **Export**: PDF, DOCX, HTML, Markdown, plain text, and JSON, from the same menu used elsewhere in
  Studio, with no watermark on any plan. See [Export Pipeline](/docs/architecture/pdf-generation)
  for how the export dispatcher handles each document type.
- **AI**: cover letter generation is one of the supported AI actions, with the same Standard/Expert
  mode and credit transparency as everywhere else.

<Callout type="info" title="Only two document types are buildable today">
  Resume and cover letter are the only document types the builder supports. The `Document` model
  also defines `PORTFOLIO` and `LINK_IN_BIO`, but portfolios are a separate product surface with
  their own editor, and link-in-bio is not built.
</Callout>

## Frequently asked questions

<Accordions>
  <Accordion id="need-cover-letter" title="Do I still need a cover letter?">
    Often, yes. Many postings ignore it, but some ask for one, and a short letter that ties your
    experience to the role helps when your resume alone does not make the fit obvious. The evidence
    is weighed in [do you need a cover
    letter?](https://blog.veriworkly.com/do-you-need-a-cover-letter)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion id="ai-cover-letter" title="Can AI write my cover letter?">
    Yes. Paste the job post into the letter and run the AI cover letter action. It spends AI
    credits, shows the cost first, and gives you a draft to review rather than overwriting your
    text. See the [AI Writing Assistant](/docs/product/ai-writing-assistant).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion id="free-cover-letter" title="Is the cover letter builder free?">
    Yes. Every template and every export format is free, with no watermark. A free account holds one
    active cover letter at a time, alongside one resume; deleting a letter frees the slot. A paid
    plan lifts that limit once payments open at launch.
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>

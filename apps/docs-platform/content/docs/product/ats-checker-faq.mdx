**The VeriWorkly ATS checker is free. Its core scan is a rules engine, not AI, so it costs no
credits. Without an account you get one scan every 48 hours on the public checker; a free account
gets two a day in Studio. Its upload reads PDF, DOCX, text, Markdown and JSON files up to 5 MB.** The answers below
cover the questions people ask most. The full feature guide is [ATS checker](/docs/product/ats-checker).

<Accordions>
  <Accordion id="is-it-free" title="Is the ATS checker free?">
    Yes. The core scan uses no AI and spends no credits, only one scan from your allowance. You can
    run it on the public checker without an account, or in Studio with a free account. Only the
    optional AI analysis and the AI resume conversion spend credits, and Studio shows the price
    before you run either one.
  </Accordion>

<Accordion id="account" title="Do I need an account to check my resume?">
  No. The public checker at [veriworkly.com/ats-checker](https://veriworkly.com/ats-checker) works
  without signing in and gives you a score and a verdict. Signing in gets the full report: the
  rule-by-rule breakdown, keyword lists and the recovered work history table. The ATS page inside
  Studio asks you to sign in before it scans.
</Accordion>

<Accordion id="how-many" title="How many free ATS scans do I get?">
  Without an account, one scan every 48 hours per connection. A free account gets two scans every 24
  hours. An account that holds AI credits, a paid plan or a pass gets 300 a month. Uploading a file
  has its own separate allowance, so reading your file never uses up a scan.
</Accordion>

<Accordion id="file-types" title="Which file types can the ATS checker read?">
  The upload accepts PDF, DOCX, plain text, Markdown and JSON, up to 5 MB; the API also accepts
  HTML. RTF is not accepted, and old Word `.doc` files should be saved as DOCX first. You can also
  paste the text, or pick a resume you built in Studio. Uploaded PDF and Word files are checked for
  tables; only PDFs are checked for columns, because a column split can only be measured on a
  laid-out page.
</Accordion>

<Accordion id="blank-pdf" title="Why does my resume PDF parse as blank?">
  The PDF has no text layer: it is a scan, a photo, or a design export that turned text into shapes.
  The checker then says the file "appears to be a scanned image or flattened graphic". A real ATS
  cannot read it either. Export a text-based PDF or DOCX. See [why some resume PDFs parse as
  blank](https://blog.veriworkly.com/why-some-resume-pdfs-parse-as-blank).
</Accordion>

<Accordion id="ai" title="Does the ATS checker use AI to score my resume?">
  No. The score comes from a deterministic rules engine, the open-source, MIT-licensed VeriWorkly
  ATS Engine, so the same resume always gets the same score. AI is a separate, optional analysis
  that explains gaps and suggests fixes. [How the ATS checker actually
  works](https://blog.veriworkly.com/how-the-ats-checker-actually-works) walks through both layers.
</Accordion>

<Accordion id="score" title="Does a high ATS score mean I will get an interview?">
  No. The score measures whether software can read your resume and how well it covers a job post. A
  person still decides. Treat a low parsing score as urgent, because job titles and dates may reach
  the employer empty. [ATS resume myths: what the evidence
  shows](https://blog.veriworkly.com/ats-resume-myths-what-the-evidence-shows) covers what an ATS
  really filters.
</Accordion>

<Accordion id="format" title="What resume format passes an ATS?">
  A single-column file with real text, standard headings such as Experience, Education and Skills on
  their own lines, and no information hidden in images, tables or text boxes. The checker tests each
  of these. The full checklist and a two-minute self-test are in [ATS-friendly resume
  format](https://blog.veriworkly.com/ats-friendly-resume-format).
</Accordion>

<Accordion id="columns" title="Do two-column resumes fail ATS checks?">
  They often read out of order. For an uploaded PDF, the checker measures how many lines are split
  across a column gap, and for an uploaded PDF or Word file it counts tables, because both scramble
  the extracted text. Pasted text and Studio documents have no page geometry, so those two checks
  are left out rather than counted against you.
</Accordion>

<Accordion id="job-description" title="Should I add a job description to the scan?">
  Yes, if you are applying for a specific role. With a job description, the report adds a job-match
  score that checks which of the post's requirements your resume covers. Requirements count more
  than nice-to-haves, and company boilerplate is ignored. The job description can be up to 20,000
  characters.
</Accordion>

  <Accordion id="quota" title="What happens when I run out of ATS scans?">
    The checker tells you when your scans come back, for example "ATS scan quota exceeded. Resets in
    1d 4h." Nothing is lost. Wait for the reset, or sign in and scan in Studio, where a free account
    gets two scans every 24 hours. See [Quota exceeded](/docs/troubleshooting/quota-exceeded).
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>

A full affiliate dashboard lives in Studio across `/affiliate`, `/affiliate/tiers`,
`/affiliate/referrals`, `/affiliate/commissions`, `/affiliate/payouts`, and
`/affiliate/leaderboard`.

<Callout type="info" title="Feature-flag gated">
  The program sits behind an `AFFILIATE_PROGRAM_ENABLED` flag. The server
  (`config.growth.affiliateProgramEnabled`), Studio (`apps/studio/lib/feature-flags.ts`) and the
  marketing site (`apps/site/lib/feature-flags.ts`) each read their own copy at startup, so set it
  in every app. It defaults to **enabled outside production** and **disabled in production** unless
  explicitly turned on. When off, the affiliate API returns `503` and Studio's `/affiliate` route
  shows a "coming soon" screen. Changing it requires a restart of the app that reads it.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warn" title="Commissions start with payments">
  Commissions are earned only on purchases, and payments are admin-only in production until launch.
  Until then no referred customer can buy, so no commission can accrue.
</Callout>

## Enrolment states

| State            | Behaviour                                                                                          |
| :--------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Not enrolled** | Shows an Enrol button, with explicit copy that signups alone earn nothing: only paid purchases do. |
| **Pending**      | Set only by an admin. The dashboard shows, but the affiliate cannot withdraw.                      |
| **Active**       | Enrolling makes an account active straight away. Referral link live, commissions accruing.         |
| **Suspended**    | Existing earnings stay visible; new activity and withdrawals are disabled.                         |

## Commission tiers

A three-tier commission structure. Rates are stored in basis points and apply to every payment a
referred customer makes, net of tax, at the affiliate's tier when the payment lands.

| Tier     | Name    | Rate             | Required conversions | Perks                               |
| :------- | :------ | :--------------- | :------------------- | :---------------------------------- |
| `TIER_1` | Starter | **2%** (200 bps) | 0                    | Monthly leaderboard access          |
| `TIER_2` | Growth  | **3%** (300 bps) | 10                   | Priority payout review              |
| `TIER_3` | Partner | **5%** (500 bps) | 50                   | Priority support, partner campaigns |

<Callout type="info" title="Tier promotion is automatic">
  An affiliate is promoted when their count of converted referrals with at least one released
  commission reaches the next threshold. Pending or reversed commissions do not count. Promotion
  only ever moves up. An admin can also set the tier in the affiliate console at
  `/admin/affiliates`; that decision holds until the affiliate crosses the next threshold.
</Callout>

## Wallet and payouts

- **Balances**: pending, available and paid, tracked in USD cents.
- **Commission review**: every commission starts `PENDING`. An admin releases it to the available
  balance (`AVAILABLE`) or reverses it.
- **Withdrawable amount**: the available balance minus any claw-back owed from a refunded
  commission.
- **Minimum withdrawal**: **$25.00** (2,500 cents).
- **Payout request flow**: an active affiliate requests a withdrawal (one open request at a time);
  an admin approves, rejects, or marks it paid.

<Callout type="info" title="Why payouts are manual">
  Dodo Payments provides no payout or transfer API, only a read-only settlements list. Payouts are
  therefore always executed **out-of-band** (bank transfer or equivalent) and then recorded in the
  system. A commission can only be marked `PAID` by an admin action.
</Callout>

Withdrawal states are `REQUESTED`, `APPROVED`, `REJECTED`, and `PAID`. Commission states are
`PENDING`, `AVAILABLE`, `REVERSED`, and `PAID`.

## Public leaderboard

Monthly and all-time boards, explicitly scoped to **released and paid commissions only**: pending
or reversed commissions never appear. Leaderboard results are cached for 5 minutes; the affiliate
dashboard for 1 minute.

## Program terms

Surfaced on the public marketing page at
[veriworkly.com/affiliate](https://veriworkly.com/affiliate):

- **Self-referral is blocked.**
- **One referral per account for life**: a user can only ever be attributed to one affiliate.
- **Attribution window**: a referral code must be applied within 30 days of the referred user's
  sign-up (`AFFILIATE_ATTRIBUTION_DAYS`) and before their first purchase. Once attached, it does not
  expire.
- **No brand-keyword bidding**: affiliates may not bid on VeriWorkly's own brand keywords in paid
  advertising.

## Data model

`AffiliateReferral` (signed up / converted / rejected), `AffiliateClick`, `AffiliateCommission`
(with a basis-points rate and a cent amount), `AffiliateWallet`, and `AffiliateWithdrawal`. See
[Database Schema](/docs/architecture/database-schema#growth-programs).

Affiliate fields also live directly on `User`: `affiliateStatus`, `affiliateTier`, a unique
`affiliateCode`, and `affiliateEnrolledAt`.

**VeriWorkly is free to use for resumes, cover letters and exports. Paid plans, passes and credit
packs add AI credits and premium portfolio features once payments open at launch. AI Credits and the
Job Hunter Bundle grant 1,000 credits a month, monthly or annual, and unused credits never roll
over. There are no free trials.** Current prices are on the [pricing
page](https://veriworkly.com/pricing).

Two independent systems work together: **entitlements**, which unlock features, and the **AI credit
wallet**, which pays for AI actions.

<Callout type="warn" title="Payments open at launch">
  Payments are closed in production today: only administrators can check out. The plans, passes and
  packs below describe the model at launch. The [pricing page](https://veriworkly.com/pricing) shows
  the same notice.
</Callout>

## What does a paid plan unlock?

Named capability grants, checked individually wherever a feature is gated:

| Entitlement         | Unlocks                                                                             |
| :------------------ | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `ai_credits`        | Marks an AI Credits or Job Hunter Bundle plan. AI actions themselves spend credits. |
| `portfolio_publish` | Publishing and rendering the premium portfolio templates (Nimbus, Cipher).          |
| `seo_controls`      | Portfolio SEO meta title, description, and social share image.                      |
| `analytics`         | Portfolio visitor analytics.                                                        |
| `watermark_removal` | Removing the "Built with VeriWorkly" badge from a published portfolio.              |

Publishing a portfolio on a free template (Signal, Atelier) at
`portfolio.veriworkly.com/portfolio/{name}` needs no entitlement. A free portfolio carries the badge.

Each grant carries a **source** (`SUBSCRIPTION`, `MANUAL`, `PROMOTION`, or `SYSTEM`) and its own
start/end window. Because access checks read entitlements rather than plan names, a manual admin
grant, a promotion, and a paid subscription all behave identically.

<Callout type="info" title="Several limits key off entitlements, not plans">
  The free-tier one-document-per-type cap and the profile-import limits both lift for anyone holding
  **either** `ai_credits` **or** `portfolio_publish`. A credit pack grants neither, so it lifts
  neither.
</Callout>

AI actions are gated by the credit balance, not by an entitlement: any account with credits, from a
plan, a pass or a pack, can spend them.

### Included AI credits

| Product           | Monthly                                    | Annual                               |
| :---------------- | :----------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------- |
| AI Credits        | 1,000 credits a month                      | 1,000 credits a month, for 12 months |
| Job Hunter Bundle | 1,000 credits a month                      | 1,000 credits a month, for 12 months |

<Callout type="warn" title="An interval is only purchasable if its product ID is configured">
  Each plan/interval pair maps to a Dodo Payments product ID supplied by environment variable. The
  public catalog reports only the intervals whose product ID is actually set, so what appears on the
  pricing page depends on deployment configuration, not just on this table.
</Callout>

## What are the Sprint and Hunt passes?

One-time purchases with no subscription, sold under the `bundle` product key. A pass does not
renew, and it acts as the Job Hunter Bundle while it is active.

| Pass                  | Interval key | Price | Duration | Credits |
| :-------------------- | :----------- | :---- | :------- | :------ |
| **3-Day Sprint Pass** | `one_day`    | $2.99 | 3 days   | 150     |
| **7-Day Hunt Pass**   | `seven_day`  | $5.99 | 7 days   | 400     |

Pass-granted credits expire when the pass window ends. A premium portfolio published during a pass
moves to a free template as soon as the pass ends, with no grace period.

## Can I buy extra AI credits?

Yes, as one-time credit packs.

For extra credits without changing a subscription:

| Pack                | Key                | Credits | Price | Expiry                |
| :------------------ | :----------------- | :------ | :---- | :-------------------- |
| 250 extra credits   | `credit_pack_250`  | 250     | $2.99 | 90 days from purchase |
| 500 extra credits   | `credit_pack_500`  | 500     | $4.99 | 90 days from purchase |
| 1,200 extra credits | `credit_pack_1200` | 1,200   | $9.99 | 90 days from purchase |

Packs are AI fuel only. They do not lift the document limit or unlock any Pro feature. Pack credits
keep their own 90-day validity and are not reset by a subscription's monthly reset.

Pack **prices are set on the corresponding Dodo Payments product**; the server's
`CREDIT_PACK_*_PRICE_CENTS` variables only set the price it displays, so keep the two in step.

## How are AI credits spent?

A full ledger, not a counter.

- **`CreditWallet`**: current balance, reserved amount, and lifetime credited/debited totals.
- **`CreditGrant`**: each batch of credits with its own expiry date.
- **`CreditReservation`**: a two-phase reserve → commit/release record for an in-flight AI call, so
  a failed generation does not burn credits.
- **`CreditTransaction`**: the user-visible history: action, reason, amount, resulting balance.
- **`CreditUsageAllocation`**: which grant(s) funded each transaction.

<Callout type="info" title="FIFO multi-grant allocation">
  A debit does not simply subtract from one balance. `CreditUsageAllocation` records exactly which
  grant(s) funded each transaction on a **FIFO-by-expiry** basis, so a user holding a subscription
  grant plus a leftover top-up is billed from the soonest-to-expire grant first. The balance you are
  about to lose is spent before the one you are not.
</Callout>

Reserved credits are not spendable until the reservation is committed or released. One credit pool
pays for every AI feature in every app. AI actions have separate **Standard** and **Expert** cost
tiers, defined in the private AI policy rather than in application code. See
[AI Writing Assistant](/docs/product/ai-writing-assistant).

## How do payments, cancellation and failed payments work?

**Dodo Payments** is the payment provider. VeriWorkly does not use Stripe anywhere.

- **Webhook-driven and idempotent.** Every webhook is recorded in `BillingWebhookEvent` keyed by the
  provider's event ID, with a processing status and retry count, so duplicate delivery cannot
  double-grant credits or entitlements. This table also powers the user-facing billing history list.
- **A last-webhook timestamp** on the subscription keeps out-of-order delivery from corrupting state.
- **Processing a payment event** grants entitlements and credits, updates portfolio publish access,
  and triggers affiliate commissions.
- **No free trials** on any plan. Subscriptions charge at checkout for the period chosen.
- **Cancellation** happens in the Dodo customer portal, opened from **Manage subscription** on
  `/billing`. A cancelled plan keeps its access to the end of the paid period.
- **Failed payment or pause.** Plan access ends and the account gets free limits. A premium
  portfolio stays online in a grace period (`PORTFOLIO_GRACE_DAYS`, default 7) while Dodo retries
  the card.
- **Lapsed portfolios stay online.** When paid access ends for good, the hourly portfolio access
  job moves a premium portfolio to the closest free template (Nimbus to Atelier, Cipher to Signal),
  with the badge.

## Frequently asked questions

<Accordions>
  <Accordion id="rollover" title="Do unused AI credits roll over to the next month?">
    No. Subscription credits reset each month: the 1,000 you did not use expire when the next 1,000
    arrive, on monthly and annual plans alike. Credit packs are separate. Pack credits last 90 days
    from purchase and are not affected by the monthly reset.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion id="trial" title="Is there a free trial?">
    No. No plan has a trial period, and a subscription charges at checkout for the period you
    choose. The free tier is the way to try VeriWorkly: every template, every export format, the
    core ATS scan and free portfolio publishing (at launch) cost nothing.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion id="cancel" title="How do I cancel my subscription?">
    Open `/billing` in Studio and choose Manage subscription. That opens the Dodo Payments customer
    portal, where you cancel. A cancelled plan keeps its access until the end of the period you paid
    for, and a premium portfolio then moves to a free template rather than going offline.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion id="pack-pro" title="Does a credit pack unlock Pro features?">
    No. Packs are AI fuel only. They add credits that last 90 days, but they do not lift the
    one-document limit, unlock premium portfolio templates, or remove the badge. Those come with
    Creator Pro, the Job Hunter Bundle or a pass.
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>

For how credits are reserved, spent and refunded, read [inside the AI credit
system](https://blog.veriworkly.com/inside-the-ai-credit-system).

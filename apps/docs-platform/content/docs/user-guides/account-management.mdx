VeriWorkly separates **who you are** (your account) from **your career data** (your Master Profile).
Understanding that split explains most of how the product behaves.

---

## Account vs. Master Profile

### Your account

Manages authentication and application preferences:

- **Email** — the address used for your passwordless sign-in.
- **Display name** and **profile image**.
- **Username** — required before you can create a public share link, since share URLs are
  username-scoped (`/share/{username}/{slug}`).
- **Preferences** — theme (light, dark, or follow system) and the auto-sync toggle.

<Callout type="warn" title="A username is permanent">
  You can only set a username once. It is baked into every share URL you have handed out — and, on a
  plan with `custom_subdomain`, into your published portfolio's address — so the API rejects a
  change with `409 Username is locked and cannot be changed`. Your input is normalised before it is
  stored (lowercased, non-alphanumerics folded to hyphens, truncated to 32 characters), so check
  what you will actually get before committing.
</Callout>

### Your Master Profile

The canonical record of your career history — work experience, education, projects, skills,
certifications, awards, publications, languages, interests, volunteer work, references, and
achievements — stored as a single record per user.

Every new resume, cover letter, or portfolio auto-seeds from it. **Editing a document never writes
back to the Master Profile**, so you can tailor one resume for one job without touching your
canonical data or any other document.

It is edited in Studio at `/profile/master`, with a raw-JSON editor at `/profile/advanced` for bulk
changes. See [Master Profile](/docs/product/master-profile) for the full behaviour.

## Signing in

VeriWorkly is passwordless. There is no account password to forget or reuse.

1. **Email OTP** — enter your email, receive a one-time passcode, and enter it. The code is valid for
   **5 minutes** and allows **3** failed attempts before it must be re-requested.
2. **OAuth** — sign in with **Google**, **GitHub**, or **LinkedIn** instead. Account linking is
   enabled, so signing in with Google after having used email OTP lands you on the same account
   rather than creating a duplicate.

Sessions last 30 days by default and are shared across VeriWorkly subdomains, so a login in Studio
carries over to the portfolio builder.

<Callout type="info" title="You do not need an account to build">
  Documents work fully logged out — they live in your browser, backed by a 30-day guest cookie. An
  account is only required for cloud sync, sharing, AI, ATS scanning, imports, and portfolio
  publishing.
</Callout>

You will also receive automatic emails on account events: a welcome email on signup, and a
**new-device login alert** whenever a session is created from an unfamiliar device, showing the
provider, IP, and user agent.

## Cloud sync

Once signed in, documents sync in the background.

- **Auto-sync toggle** — on by default, switchable at `/settings`, alongside a manual **Sync Now**
  action.
- **Per-document opt-out** — mark any single document "keep local only" to exclude it from sync
  entirely.
- **Sync status** — every document shows one of `local-only`, `pending`, `syncing`, `synced`, or
  `conflicted`.

### Conflict handling

VeriWorkly uses **optimistic concurrency**, not last-write-wins. Each document carries a revision
counter; if the cloud copy has moved on since your device last synced, the write is **rejected** and
the document is marked `conflicted` rather than overwritten. Resolve it from the conflict details
view at `/settings`.

<Callout type="info" title="You can keep typing during a sync">
  When a sync request is in flight, the response only updates the document's sync metadata — never
  its content. Edits you make while waiting are not overwritten when the request lands.
</Callout>

## Settings

`/settings` in Studio contains:

- **Appearance** — light, dark, or follow-system theme.
- **Sync** — the auto-sync toggle, manual sync, and conflict resolution.
- **Developer API keys** — a link through to `/api-keys`. See [API Keys](/docs/api-keys).

## Deleting your data

**Individual documents** can be deleted at any time from the dashboard. Deletion is a soft delete
server-side: the record is flagged rather than immediately purged, and it stops counting against the
free-tier one-document-per-type limit.

**Local data** persists in your browser until you clear site data or delete the document. Clearing
browser storage removes anything that was never synced.

<Callout type="warn" title="Self-service account deletion is not yet available">
  Studio does not currently expose an account-deletion control, and the backend's delete-user
  endpoint is not enabled. To have your account and all associated cloud data removed, email
  <a href="mailto:info@veriworkly.com">info@veriworkly.com</a>. You will receive a confirmation
  email when the deletion is processed. Deletion cascades to your documents, Master Profile, share
  links, portfolio, API keys, and sessions.
</Callout>

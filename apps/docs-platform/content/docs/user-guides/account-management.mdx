**Your VeriWorkly account is passwordless: sign in with a one-time email code or with Google, GitHub
or LinkedIn. Manage your name and username on `/profile`, sync and appearance on `/settings`, and
delete the account from the Danger Zone on `/profile`. Your career history lives separately, in
your Master Profile.** Open your account at [app.veriworkly.com/profile](https://app.veriworkly.com/profile).

---

## What is the difference between my account and my Master Profile?

Your account is who you are and how you sign in. Your Master Profile is your career history.

### Your account

Manages authentication and application preferences:

- **Email**: the address used for your passwordless sign-in.
- **Display name** and **profile image**.
- **Username**: required before you can create a public share link, since share URLs are
  username-scoped (`/share/{username}/{slug}`).
- **Preferences**: theme (light, dark, or follow system) and the auto-sync toggle.

<Callout type="warn" title="A username is permanent">
  You can only set a username once. It is part of every share URL you have handed out, so the API
  rejects a change with `409 Username is locked and cannot be changed`. Your input is normalised
  before it is stored: accents are stripped, letters are lowercased, anything other than `a-z`,
  `0-9`, `_` and `-` becomes a hyphen, and the result is cut to 32 characters. It must be at least 3
  characters and not a reserved name. Check what you will actually get before committing.
</Callout>

### Your Master Profile

The canonical record of your career history (work experience, education, projects, skills,
certifications, awards, publications, languages, interests, volunteer work, references, and
achievements), stored as a single record per user.

Every new resume, cover letter, or portfolio auto-seeds from it. **Editing a document never writes
back to the Master Profile**, so you can tailor one resume for one job without touching your
canonical data or any other document.

It is edited in Studio at `/profile/master`, with a raw-JSON editor at `/profile/advanced` for bulk
changes. See [Master Profile](/docs/product/master-profile) for the full behaviour.

## How do I sign in?

VeriWorkly is passwordless. There is no account password to forget or reuse.

1. **Email OTP**: enter your email, receive a one-time passcode, and enter it. The code is valid for
   **5 minutes** and allows **3** failed attempts before it must be re-requested.
2. **OAuth**: sign in with **Google**, **GitHub**, or **LinkedIn** instead. Account linking is
   enabled, so signing in with Google after having used email OTP lands you on the same account
   rather than creating a duplicate.

<Callout type="warn" title="One GitHub account, linked for good">
  An account can have one linked GitHub account, and it cannot be disconnected or swapped for
  another. Its email does not have to match your VeriWorkly email. Free accounts import only from
  the linked GitHub; paid plans can import any public GitHub username.
</Callout>

Sessions last 30 days by default and are shared across VeriWorkly subdomains, so a login in Studio
carries over to the portfolio builder.

<Callout type="info" title="You do not need an account to build">
  No login required: choose **Continue as Guest** on the sign-in screen. Your documents live in your
  browser, and a 30-day guest cookie keeps you in guest mode. Sign in for cloud sync, share links,
  and the Master Profile.
</Callout>

You will also receive automatic emails on account events: a welcome email on signup, and a
**new-device login alert** whenever a session is created from an unfamiliar device, showing the
provider, IP, and user agent.

## How does cloud sync work?

Once signed in, documents sync in the background.

- **Auto-sync toggle**: on by default, switchable at `/settings`, alongside a manual **Sync Now**
  action.
- **Per-document opt-out**: mark any single document "keep local only" to exclude it from sync
  entirely.
- **Sync status**: every document shows one of `local-only`, `pending`, `syncing`, `synced`, or
  `conflicted`.

### Conflict handling

VeriWorkly uses **optimistic concurrency**, not last-write-wins. Each document carries a revision
counter; if the cloud copy has moved on since your device last synced, the write is **rejected** and
the document is marked `conflicted` rather than overwritten. Resolve it from the conflict details
view at `/settings`.

<Callout type="info" title="You can keep typing during a sync">
  When a sync request is in flight, the response only updates the document's sync metadata, never
  its content. Edits you make while waiting are not overwritten when the request lands.
</Callout>

## Settings

`/settings` in Studio contains:

- **Appearance**: light, dark, or follow-system theme.
- **Sync**: the auto-sync toggle, manual sync, and conflict resolution.
- **Developer API keys**: a link through to `/api-keys`. See [API Keys](/docs/api-keys).

## Your profile page

`/profile` in Studio shows your account:

- **Name**: edit your display name, up to 255 characters.
- **Username**: set it once. After that it shows as locked.
- **Account details**: your email and whether it is verified, the auto-sync setting, and when the
  account was created and last updated.
- **Account stats**: how many API keys, share links, and documents your account holds.
- **Links** to Billing, AI credit usage, the affiliate program, the Master Profile, Advanced JSON,
  and API keys.
- **Danger Zone**: delete your account. See below.

## How do I delete my documents or my account?

**Individual documents** can be deleted at any time from the dashboard. Deletion is a soft delete
server-side: the record is flagged rather than immediately purged, and it stops counting against the
free-tier one-document-per-type limit.

**Local data** persists in your browser until you clear site data or delete the document. Clearing
browser storage removes anything that was never synced.

**Deleted documents** are wiped from the server 30 days after you delete them.

**Your account** can be deleted from the **Danger Zone** on `/profile`. Type your email to confirm.
Before you do, use **Download my data** in the same dialog: it saves one JSON file with every
document in your account and your Master Profile.

Deletion is permanent. It removes your documents, Master Profile, share links, portfolios and their
uploaded images, API keys, sessions, AI credits, and affiliate data. The documents stored on that
device are removed too. A confirmation email follows.

<Callout type="warn" title="When deletion is refused">
  The server answers `409` and deletes nothing while:

- a subscription can still charge you. That includes one you have cancelled whose paid period has
  not ended, and one that is paused or retrying a failed payment. Cancel it from **Billing**
  (Manage Subscription), wait for it to end, then delete. VeriWorkly cannot cancel it for you,
  because it lives with the payment provider.
- a checkout is still open. Finish or cancel it first.
- an affiliate withdrawal is requested or approved but not yet paid.
- you have unpaid affiliate commissions. The message states the amount you would forfeit. Sending
  the request again with `{ "confirmAffiliateForfeit": true }` confirms it.

</Callout>

**Open your profile page in Studio ([app.veriworkly.com/profile](https://app.veriworkly.com/profile)),
select Delete Account in the Danger Zone, download your data, type your email address, and confirm.
Deletion is permanent and runs straight away. A live or recently cancelled subscription, an open
checkout or a pending affiliate withdrawal blocks it until that is settled.**

## How do I delete my account?

You delete your account yourself from the Studio profile page. You must be signed in. The steps take
about a minute, and the last one cannot be undone. Download your data before you confirm, because
nothing can be restored afterwards.

<Steps>
  <Step>
    Sign in to [app.veriworkly.com](https://app.veriworkly.com) and open **Profile** (`/profile`).
  </Step>
  <Step>In the **Danger Zone** card, select **Delete Account**.</Step>
  <Step>
    Select **Download my data** to save one JSON file with every document in your account and your
    Master Profile.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Type the email address of your account in the confirmation box. Capital letters do not matter.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Select **Permanently Delete Everything**. Studio signs you out, clears this browser's copy of
    your documents, and takes you to the VeriWorkly home page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How do I download my data before deleting?

Use **Download my data** in the delete dialog. It saves one JSON file with every document in your
account and your Master Profile. To restore later, an editor's **Import JSON** brings back a document
and **Profile > Advanced JSON > Import** brings back the Master Profile. For a PDF, open the document
and download it.

The dialog also counts the documents on this device and how many were never saved to the cloud.
Deleting your account removes them from this device too. If the full download fails, the dialog
offers **Download local documents only**, which saves just the documents on this device.

## What gets deleted?

Your account and everything tied to it: documents, the Master Profile, share links, your published
portfolio and its uploaded images, API keys, sessions, and affiliate and ambassador records. Unused AI
credits, credit packs and grants are lost without a refund. API keys stop working at once, and every
signed-in device is signed out.

## What does VeriWorkly keep after deletion?

Billing records are kept, without your personal details. Subscriptions, credit history and payment
provider events stay on file as long as tax law requires, detached from your account. The personal
data the payment provider sent in those events is redacted. Copies may also remain in backups until
those backups expire.

After the deletion, VeriWorkly sends a confirmation email with the subject **Your VeriWorkly Account
Has Been Deleted** to the address on the account.

## Why can't I delete my account?

The server refuses a deletion that would leave money or a charge unaccounted for. Each refusal tells
you what to do. The table lists the exact messages and the fix for each.

| Message you see                                                                                                     | What to do                                                                                                                                          |
| :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Cancel your subscription before deleting your account: open Billing and use Manage Subscription. ...                | Open [Billing](https://app.veriworkly.com/billing), select **Manage subscription**, and cancel. Delete your account once the paid period has ended. |
| Your subscription is cancelled and ends on _date_. You can delete your account once it has ended. ...               | Wait until that date, or contact support to have it deleted sooner.                                                                                 |
| A checkout is still open for this account. Finish or cancel it before deleting the account.                         | Finish or cancel the checkout on the Billing page. An open checkout stays payable for up to 24 hours.                                               |
| You have a withdrawal in progress. Wait for it to be paid or rejected before deleting your account.                 | Wait until the affiliate payout is paid or rejected.                                                                                                |
| You have _amount_ USD in unpaid affiliate commissions. Deleting your account forfeits it. Confirm to delete anyway. | See the next section.                                                                                                                               |
| Account deletion is temporarily unavailable. Try again shortly.                                                     | Try again in a few minutes.                                                                                                                         |

A subscription blocks deletion while the payment provider can still charge it: active, paused, on
hold or past due. VeriWorkly cannot cancel it for you, because it lives with the payment provider.
Payments are admin-only in production today, so this applies once payments open at launch.

## What if I have unpaid affiliate commissions?

Deleting your account forfeits unpaid affiliate commissions. Studio shows the amount, split into
available and pending, and the delete button changes to **Forfeit _amount_ and delete**. To keep the
money, cancel and request a payout first. If the balance grows before you confirm, Studio asks again
at the new amount.

## Can I delete my account with an API key?

No. Account deletion only works from a signed-in Studio session. A request to `DELETE /users/me` with
an API key is refused with `403`, because the scope that renames an account must not be able to
destroy it. See [Delete the current user](/api-reference/users/deleteCurrentUser) in the API
reference.

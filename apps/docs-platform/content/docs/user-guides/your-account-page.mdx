**Your account page is Profile in Studio, at
[app.veriworkly.com/profile](https://app.veriworkly.com/profile). It shows your name, username,
email, automatic backup setting and account dates, counts your documents, share links and API keys,
links to billing, credits and developer tools, and holds the Danger Zone for deleting your account.
Your Master Profile is edited on its own page.**

## What is on my account page?

The page has four parts. Each one is described below. In guest mode the page opens, but it shows
"Local builder" and "No account connected", the name and username cards are switched off until you
sign in, and the Danger Zone is hidden.

- **Account details**: name, username, email, verification, backup setting, created and updated
  dates.
- **Account stats**: API keys, share links and documents.
- **Links**: Billing, credits, the affiliate program, Master Profile, Advanced JSON and API keys.
- **Danger Zone**: account deletion.

## How do I change my name?

Select the pencil next to your name, or the **Name** card, type the new name and save. The name
cannot be empty and can be up to 255 characters. This is your account's display name: changing it
does not change your Master Profile or the name printed on any document.

## How do I set my username?

Select the **Username** card while it says **Configure**. Usernames are 3 to 32 characters of
lowercase letters, numbers, hyphens and underscores. Studio only saves a name it has confirmed is
available, and shows the exact value it will save if your input needs changing. You need a username before you can
create a share link.

## Can I change my username?

No. A username is locked once it is set, because it is part of every share link you have handed
out. Your portfolio address is the subdomain you choose in the portfolio settings, not your username. Selecting the card then shows **Username Locked**. If you have an exceptional
reason to change it, use the **contact us** link in that dialog to reach the VeriWorkly team.

## What do the account stats count?

**Account stats** shows three numbers. **Documents** counts your resumes and cover letters in your
account, not deleted ones and not portfolios. **Share links** counts links on documents that still
exist. **API keys** counts the keys on your account. Documents kept only on this device are not
counted.

## Where are billing, credits and API keys?

The links grid on the page goes to each tool:

| Link              | Opens               | What it is for                                                |
| :---------------- | :------------------ | :------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Billing and plan  | `/billing`          | Your plan, renewals, billing activity and extra credits       |
| AI credit usage   | `/credits`          | Balance, expiry, action costs and usage history               |
| Affiliate program | `/affiliate`        | Referrals, commissions, tiers and payouts                     |
| Master Profile    | `/profile/master`   | Career details reused to start new documents                  |
| Advanced JSON     | `/profile/advanced` | Inspect, import, export and repair the Master Profile as JSON |
| API keys          | `/api-keys`         | Scoped developer tokens                                       |

Purchases on the Billing page open at launch; payments are admin-only in production today.

## Where do I delete my account?

In the **Danger Zone** card at the bottom of the account page. Select **Delete Account**, download
your data, type your email address and confirm. The full steps, what is kept and what can block a
deletion are in [Delete your account](/docs/user-guides/delete-your-account).

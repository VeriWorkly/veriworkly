**"Not enough AI credits" means your credit balance is lower than the cost of the AI action you
started, so nothing ran and nothing was charged. AI writing, the AI ATS analysis, resume conversion
and AI-assisted imports all spend credits. Add credits from the Billing page. Payments open at launch;
they are admin-only in production today.**

## Not enough AI credits. Credits come with a credit pack, a pass, AI Credits or the Job Hunter Bundle.

This is the API's message, returned with status `402`. Studio shortens it to "Not enough credits."
and shows a **Get credits** or **Buy credits** link to the Billing page. Your balance and the cost of
each action are on the AI credit usage page in Studio (`/credits`).

## How do I get more AI credits?

Credits come with a credit pack, a 3-Day Sprint Pass or 7-Day Hunt Pass, an AI Credits plan, or the
Job Hunter Bundle. A credit pack adds credits only; it does not unlock Pro features. All of these are
bought, and purchases open at launch. Until then the Billing page says "Payments open soon. Purchases
are not available yet; nothing has been charged."

See [Billing and credits](/docs/product/billing-and-credits) for how credits are granted and when
they expire, and [Launch status](/docs/product/launch-status) for what works for free today.

## Does the ATS checker need credits?

The core ATS scan does not. It is a rules engine and costs no credits, only a scan from your
allowance. The AI analysis and the AI resume conversion on the Studio ATS page spend credits, and
Studio shows the price next to each one. See
[Quota exceeded](/docs/troubleshooting/quota-exceeded) for the scan allowance.

**Free accounts and guest mode keep one active resume and one active cover letter at a time. The
message appears when you try to create, duplicate or import a second one. Edit or delete the document
you already have. Unlimited documents come with any paid plan or a pass, which you can buy once
payments open at launch.**

## Free accounts can keep 1 active resume at a time.

This is the API's message, returned with status `402` and `details.code: DOCUMENT_LIMIT`. In full:
"Free accounts can keep 1 active resume at a time. Unlimited documents come with any plan (AI
Credits, Creator Pro or the Job Hunter Bundle) or a pass; credit packs add AI credits only." The same
message names "cover letter" for a second cover letter.

## Without a subscription or pass you can keep 1 resume at a time.

This is Studio's wording of the same limit, shown before anything is sent to the API: "Without a
subscription or pass you can keep 1 resume at a time. Creator Pro, an AI Credits subscription, the
Bundle or a 3- or 7-day pass unlock unlimited documents; credit packs do not." A guest is always on
this limit.

## How do I fix the document limit?

Work in the document you already have, or delete it to start a new one. The limit counts documents
of one type, so one resume and one cover letter can exist side by side. A document backed up to your
account that is over the limit shows the sync status **Upgrade needed** ("Over the document limit")
and stays on this device until you have room.

To keep more than one, unlimited documents come with Creator Pro, AI Credits, the Job Hunter Bundle,
or a 3-Day Sprint Pass or 7-Day Hunt Pass. A credit pack does not lift the limit. Purchases open at
launch; see [Launch status](/docs/product/launch-status).

## Does an AI action count toward the limit?

Yes, when it makes a new document. An AI resume conversion or a profile import that would create a
second resume is refused before any credits are charged, and the message names the limit rather than
offering credits, because credits do not lift it.

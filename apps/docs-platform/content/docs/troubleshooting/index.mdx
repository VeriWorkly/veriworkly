Studio, the public site or the API, says what it means, and gives the fix. Most messages come from
browser storage, a sync conflict, a usage limit, AI credits, or the payment and publishing switch
that stays off until launch.**

## What if my message is not listed?

Search these docs for a distinctive phrase from the message. If it came from deleting your account,
see [Delete your account](/docs/user-guides/delete-your-account). For anything else, email
[info@veriworkly.com](mailto:info@veriworkly.com) with the exact message, the page you were on and
the time it happened.

## Where do these messages come from?

Messages that mention "this device" come from Studio and your browser's storage. Messages with an
HTTP status, such as `402`, `403` or `429`, come from the VeriWorkly API, and Studio either shows
them as they are or rewrites them in shorter words. API integrations receive the API wording in the
`message` field of the error response.

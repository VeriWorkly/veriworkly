# VeriWorkly Pricing & Monetization Model

> Structured pricing reference for human visitors and AI agents.

**No free trials.** VeriWorkly does not offer a trial on any plan. The free tier is how
you try the product, for as long as you like. Subscriptions charge at checkout for the
period you chose, and the 3-Day Sprint Pass and 7-Day Hunt Pass are one-time purchases that
expire rather than renew. Nothing converts from free to paid on its own.

## Subscription Tiers & Passes

### 1. Free Tier

- **Price**: $0
- **Includes**:
  - Full Resume & Cover Letter Editor
  - Master Profile Store & Local Draft Storage
  - Exports: PDF, DOCX, HTML, Markdown, Plain Text, JSON (all formats, ungated, no watermark)
  - Free ATS Resume & Job Match Scanning (1 scan/48h guest, 2 scans/24h logged-in)
  - GitHub & LinkedIn profile import. Free tier: 1 GitHub import per rolling 24 hours,
    restricted to your own connected account; 1 LinkedIn import per rolling 30 days.
    Both limits are measured from your last import, not reset on a calendar boundary.
  - Portfolio publishing on a `username.veriworkly.com` subdomain using the free core
    templates (Signal, Atelier), which display a "Built with VeriWorkly" badge
- **Document limit**: 1 active document per type (resume, cover letter, portfolio,
  link-in-bio). The cap is per type, so one of each is fine. It applies to guests and
  free accounts alike; paid plans lift it.
- **AI Writing Credits**: 0 (requires pass or credit add-on)

> **Status**: payments are currently closed, so none of the paid plans below can be
> bought today; the /pricing page shows the same notice. Portfolio publishing is
> not yet enabled in production. The tiers below describe the model at launch.

### 2. 3-Day Sprint Pass

- **Price**: $2.99 (one-time)
- **Includes**:
  - 3 days of Creator Pro portfolio features on `username.veriworkly.com`
  - 150 AI Writing Credits (valid during pass)
  - Premium portfolio templates (Nimbus, Cipher), badge removal & portfolio analytics

### 3. 7-Day Hunt Pass

- **Price**: $5.99 (one-time)
- **Includes**:
  - 7 days of Creator Pro portfolio features on `username.veriworkly.com`
  - 400 AI Writing Credits (valid during pass)
  - Premium portfolio templates (Nimbus, Cipher), badge removal & portfolio analytics

### 4. Job Hunter Bundle, monthly

- **Price**: $14.99 / month
- **Includes**:
  - Creator Pro portfolio features on `username.veriworkly.com`
  - 1,000 AI Writing Credits a month; unused credits expire at the end of the month (no rollover)
  - 300 ATS Scans a month (any account with AI credits gets these, credit packs included)
  - Premium portfolio templates (Nimbus, Cipher), badge removal & full portfolio analytics
  - Higher import limits: up to 50 GitHub imports a day, and LinkedIn imports without the
    free tier's 30-day gap

### 5. Job Hunter Bundle, annual

- **Price**: $11.99 / month ($143.88 billed annually)
- **Includes**:
  - Everything in the monthly Job Hunter Bundle, with a 20% annual discount.
  - Credits are granted 1,000 a month for 12 months, not all at once; unused credits do not
    roll over, the same as the monthly plan.

### 6. Creator Pro (portfolio only)

- **Price**: $9.99 / month or $7.99 / month ($95.88 billed annually)
- **Includes**: Premium portfolio templates (Nimbus, Cipher), multi-page portfolios, portfolio badge removal, visitor analytics, and SEO controls on your `username.veriworkly.com` subdomain (no AI credits). The subdomain itself is free on every plan.

### 7. AI Credits (writing only)

- **Price**: $5.99 / month or $4.99 / month ($59.90 billed annually)
- **Includes**: 1,000 AI writing credits/month for resume tailoring and rewriting (no Creator Pro portfolio features).

## Credit Packs (One-Time Top-Ups)

- **250 AI Credits**: $2.99 (valid for 90 days)
- **500 AI Credits**: $4.99 (valid for 90 days)

## AI Action Costs

- **Standard Rewrite / Action**: 1 to 5 credits
- **Expert AI Tailoring / Job Analysis**: 10 to 25 credits

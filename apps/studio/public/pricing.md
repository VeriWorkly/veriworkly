# VeriWorkly Studio — Pricing & Entitlements Model

## Subscription Plans

- **Free Tier**: Local storage, document builder, Master Profile store, 7 resume and 5 cover letter templates, local exports (PDF/DOCX/HTML/MD/JSON), free ATS scans.
- **3-Day Sprint Pass ($2.99)**: 3-day Creator Pro subdomain hosting + 150 AI writing credits.
- **7-Day Hunt Pass ($5.99)**: 7-day Creator Pro subdomain hosting + 400 AI writing credits.
- **Monthly Pro Bundle ($14.99/mo)**: Creator Pro subdomain hosting + 1,000 monthly AI credits + 300 ATS scans/period.
- **Annual Pro Bundle ($11.99/mo eq, $143.88/yr)**: All Monthly Pro features with 20% annual discount.
- **Creator Pro Standalone ($9.99/mo or $7.99/mo eq, $95.88/yr)**: Custom subdomain hosting, multi-page portfolios, analytics, watermark removal.
- **AI Credits Standalone ($5.99/mo or $4.99/mo eq, $59.90/yr)**: 1,000 monthly AI writing credits.

## Credit Packs (Top-Ups)

- **250 AI Credits**: $2.99 (90 days validity)
- **500 AI Credits**: $4.99 (90 days validity)
